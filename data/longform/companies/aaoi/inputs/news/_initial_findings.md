# Applied Optoelectronics — initial findings (2026-05-23)

## Founder / CEO

- **Dr. Chih-Hsiang "Thompson" Lin**
- Founded Applied Optoelectronics 1997-02-28
- Chairman, President, CEO since inception (28+ years)
- BS Nuclear Engineering, National Tsing Hua University (Taiwan)
- MS, University of Missouri-Columbia
- Senior Research Scientist, University of Houston (1994-1998)
- Research Associate Professor, University of Houston (1998-2000)
- Headquarters in Sugar Land, Texas since 2000

## Business model

- Vertically integrated: Texas + Taiwan + China manufacturing
- Four product lines:
  1. Semiconductors & Laser Components (e.g., 250um LD 10G)
  2. Optical Transceivers (800G OSFP DR8, 1.6T)
  3. HFC Networking (Quantum18 amplifiers, QuantumLink management)
  4. Related optical components

## End markets

- Telecom (long haul, metro, access)
- FTTH (fiber-to-the-home)
- Sensing and measurement
- Datacenter
- Wireless infrastructure
- Cable broadband / DOCSIS

## FY2025 highlights

- **Revenue: $186.7 million** (record full year)
- Record Q4 2025 results
- Broad-based demand across CATV + datacenter
- Production capacity exit 2025: **~100,000 units/month 800G transceivers**, 35% from US
- Targeting **500,000+ units/month combined 800G + 1.6T by end-2026**

## Customer concentration
