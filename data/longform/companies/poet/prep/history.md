# POET Technologies — corporate history

## Origins: from POET Inc. to POET Technologies

POET Technologies Inc. was originally incorporated in Canada and has been publicly listed for over a decade — initially on the Toronto-listed venture exchanges, later moving to the TSX main board and eventually adding a NASDAQ dual listing. The company's earliest technology thesis (from which the POET name — Planar Opto Electronic Technology — derives) was monolithic integration of electronic and optical functions on gallium-arsenide substrates. The pre-2015 technology arc produced material IP and accumulated losses but no commercial-scale product.

## The Venkatesan pivot (2015)

Dr. Suresh Venkatesan joined as Chairman and CEO in 2015 from his most recent role as Senior Vice President of Technology Development at GlobalFoundries. Venkatesan's pedigree — Purdue PhD in EE, 25+ US patents, Freescale Fellow induction in 2007 for CMOS contributions, Motorola SABA induction in 2004, GlobalFoundries 28nm node leadership and 14nm transfer — brought semiconductor process discipline to the photonics platform thesis.

Under Venkatesan, POET pivoted from the early monolithic-integration approach to the POET Optical Interposer — a wafer-level photonic integration platform built around a passive substrate that hosts multiple optical components (lasers, modulators, photodetectors, filters) with minimal optical loss. The platform is materials-agnostic in that it can host III-V and silicon photonic components, which is structurally different from a pure silicon-photonics or a pure compound-semiconductor approach.

## The Singapore manufacturing base

POET established a Singapore subsidiary (POET Singapore Pte. Ltd.) to operate the wafer-level photonic integration line. Singapore was selected for its (a) deep semiconductor manufacturing labour pool, (b) geographic insulation from US-China trade friction relative to a China-based operation, and (c) proximity to the broader Singapore semiconductor cluster that includes Taiwan-affiliated fabs and OSAT partners. The 20-F geopolitical risk disclosure lists China, Singapore, and Malaysia as the Asia operations footprint.

## Board build-out (2017-2025)

The board through this period was assembled deliberately:
- **Jean-Louis Malinge** joined in September 2017 as a director and subsequently became Lead Independent Director. Malinge had been President and CEO of Kotura — a silicon photonics company sold to Mellanox in 2013 for $82 million. The directly comparable operating precedent was the most consequential single board addition for POET's strategic credibility.
- **Glen Riley** joined in December 2020. Riley's 32-year career across Qorvo, TriQuint (international + GaAs foundry + optoelectronics GM roles), Opticalis (CEO), Agere optoelectronics VP, and Lucent Microelectronics Asia provides operational depth at the compound-semiconductor and optoelectronics layer.
- **Theresa Lan Ende** joined as Board Advisor in June 2022 and was formally elected director at the October 2022 AGM. Her role as Arista Networks' Chief Procurement Officer placed a working hyperscaler-supply-chain customer-side credential on the board.
- **Robert (Bob) Tirva** joined in December 2024. Tirva's ex-Sonim CFO/COO + IBM/Broadcom/Dropbox career provides the public-company financial-execution credential.
- **Sohail Khan** joined in July 2025. Khan's Coherent SiC EVP role + Agere CEO + ViXS CEO + LightPath Technologies 16-year board tenure brings the deepest chip-CEO M&A pattern recognition on the board.

**Chris Tsiofas retired at the June 2025 AGM** after 13 years on the board, ending his tenure as Audit Committee Chair. The seat reconfiguration is the single most consequential governance event of 2024-2025 — Bob Tirva, who joined six months earlier with the requisite public-company-CFO credentials, is the natural successor.

## The $250M financing (2025)

POET closed a $250 million institutional equity financing in 2025 from three undisclosed institutional buyers. The transaction expanded total assets from $69.7 million (December 2024) to $328.6 million (December 2025) — a nearly 5x balance sheet expansion. Cash plus short-term investments stand at $313.4 million at year-end 2025, providing what management characterises as multi-year runway to execute the Optical Interposer platform thesis through qualification and into volume.

The financing also created the structural dilution overhang: 5.79 million options and 37.36 million warrants outstanding against 132.3 million common shares represent potential dilution of 28.35% on full exercise. The $135.6 million derivative warrant liability on the balance sheet is the non-cash mark-to-market recognition of that overhang.

## FY2025 operating results: pre-revenue, pre-qualification, pre-commercial

FY2025 reported (per 20-F filing 2026):
- Net loss $62.96 million
- R&D expense $18.08 million (incl. $2.22M stock-based comp)
- SG&A expense $25.08 million (incl. $3.89M stock-based comp, $3.32M D&A)
- Approximately $35 million of non-cash operating costs
- Q3 2025 revenue $298,434 and Q4 2025 revenue $341,202 — small NRE-and-product receipts, not commercial volume
- Total assets $328.6M, cash and short-term investments $313.4M

The 20-F explicitly notes: "No major customers, partners, or design wins explicitly named" and "limited operating history in the data center market."

## The Sivers collaboration (September 2025)

In September 2025, POET and Sivers Semiconductors announced a collaboration on external light sources for co-packaged optics and the next-generation AI market. The collaboration pairs Sivers' UK subsidiary Sivers Photonics (custom high-power InP DFB lasers) with POET's Optical Interposer to produce ELS module candidates for hyperscaler CPO supply chains. This is the most concrete disclosed strategic partnership POET has at the snapshot date.

## Where the story sits at snapshot (May 23, 2026)

POET is a pre-commercial photonics platform company with $313M cash, a directionally credible board, and no commercial revenue. The investment proposition is binary: if hyperscalers adopt co-packaged optics at scale in 2027-2028 and POET's Optical Interposer is qualified as a component in those supply chains, the platform thesis is durable. If pluggable optics continue to dominate or competing interposer architectures win (Intel's silicon photonics, Marvell's optical engines, Broadcom's CPO, Ayar Labs, Lightmatter, Celestial AI), the $313M cash buys multi-year runway but the thesis may not crystallise inside it.
