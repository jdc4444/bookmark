# Sivers Semiconductors — corporate history

## 1951 — Founding as Sivers Lab AB

- Engineer **Carl von Sivers** founds Sivers Lab AB in Sweden
- Manufactures **passive microwave products** and **slotted line VSWR detectors**
- Serves professional and educational markets

## 1960 — Acquired by Philips Sweden

- **Philips, Sweden** acquires Sivers Lab AB
- Becomes part of the Philips technology portfolio in Sweden

## (Gap to fill) — Spinout / IPO / Sivers IMA Holding era

- IMA = "Innovative Microwave Applications" (rough)

## 2017 — Sivers Photonics is born via CST Global acquisition

- **Sivers IMA Holding acquires CST Global**
- CST Global was an independent manufacturer of **III-V photonic devices**
- Provided custom foundry services in wafer / coated bar / chip device / die-on-tape formats
- Has its own R&D facility and clean room manufacturing capacity in the UK
- Renamed to **Sivers Photonics Ltd.** — becomes the company's photonics segment
- This is the foundational move that creates the modern Sivers
- Independent western source for **III-V photonic lasers** — the differentiator that drives the 2025-26 CPO thesis

## 2022 — MixComm acquisition expands wireless

- Acquires **MixComm**, a U.S.-based **millimeter-wave (mmWave) startup**
- Brings additional wireless customers and projects
- Strengthens the Sivers Wireless AB segment

## ~2022-2024 — Sivers IMA → Sivers Semiconductors rebrand

- Group consolidates under **Sivers Semiconductors AB** name
- Subsidiaries: **Sivers Wireless AB** (mmWave RF) + **Sivers Photonics Ltd.** (DFB lasers)

## 2024-08-19 — CEO transition

- **Anders Storm** (long-tenured CEO through the photonics buildout) steps down
- **Vickram Vathulya** (PhD Lehigh, MBA Berkeley Haas) appointed President & CEO
- Brings 25+ years semiconductor industry leadership: Nuvotronics (President), Maxim Semiconductors (standard products business head), NXP Semiconductors

## 2025 — Photonics breakout year

- Revenue +25% YoY to SEK 306.6M (PCAOB-restated)
- Pipeline grows 64% to $453M
- **Sept 29 2025: POET Technologies strategic collaboration** announced — External Light Source modules for CPO + AI infrastructure
- **Ayar Labs partnership** — laser arrays for SuperNova
- **O-Net/Enablence partnership** — ELS modules with thermal solutions
- SEK 95M rights issue (8% dilution)

## 2026 — Dual listing prep + accounting restatement

- **Feb 26 2026**: Preliminary Q4 / full-year 2025 results
- **Feb 2026**: Additional $17M via term loan + convertible debt
- **May 13 2026**: 2025 Annual Report finally published with **PCAOB-restated financials** — preparing for potential dual listing on Nasdaq New York
  - EBIT widened from -SEK 141M (Swedish K3) → -SEK 178M (PCAOB)
  - Revenue reallocation; inventory revaluation; share-based comp fair value; impairment of capitalized development expenditures
- **May 19 2026**: $6.6M NEMC Year 2 funding — US Defense Infrastructure modernization
- **May 20 2026**: Board nominations — Joakim Nideborn + Helena Svancar

## The long arc

A 75-year-old Stockholm passive-microwave shop became the leading independent western source of DFB lasers for AI data center co-packaged optics — by acquiring a Scottish III-V photonics specialist in 2017 and a US mmWave startup in 2022, then surviving the dilution-heavy growth-stage years to land at the moment when the AI optical-interconnect supercycle arrived. The 2024 CEO change (Storm → Vathulya, a US-educated semiconductor veteran) and the 2026 PCAOB-restated financials both point to one destination: dual listing on Nasdaq New York.
