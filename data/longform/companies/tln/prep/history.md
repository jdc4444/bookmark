# Talen Energy — corporate history

## Origins as PPL spin-off (2015)

Talen Energy Corporation was formed in 2015 as a spin-off from PPL Corporation's competitive power generation business combined with assets from Riverstone Holdings' merchant power portfolio. The original Talen Energy entity carried a multi-state generation fleet across PJM with significant exposure to wholesale electricity prices.

## The 2016 take-private and post-restructuring rebuild

In 2016 Riverstone took Talen Energy private in a leveraged buyout. The subsequent half-decade was characterised by a difficult merchant-power market — wholesale power prices in PJM had fallen significantly through the mid-to-late 2010s on the back of cheap natural gas and renewable energy growth, compressing the unhedged revenue line at IPPs like Talen.

Talen's 2022 Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing came after the financial pressures of the 2020-2022 commodity volatility (initial COVID price collapse, then Russia-Ukraine 2022 spike) combined with hedge positions and capital structure constraints. The company emerged from Chapter 11 in 2023 with a restructured balance sheet, a refocused asset base, and a new public listing on NASDAQ under the ticker TLN.

## The post-restructuring management buildout (2023-2024)

Mark "Mac" McFarland joined as CEO in May 2023, bringing his post-restructuring CEO archetype track record from California Resources Corporation, GenOn Energy, Luminant, and Energy Future Holdings. Through 2023-2024 the executive team was rebuilt with a deep cluster of EFH and GenOn alumni (Andrew Wright as Chief Admin Officer from 14 years as EFH General Counsel; Darren Olagues joining October 2024 as Chief Development Officer from ex-GenOn CFO; Brad Berryman elevated to COO from prior Chief Nuclear Officer role) plus deep nuclear depth (Edward Casulli as Chief Nuclear Officer with prior Susquehanna Site VP credentials).

The board through this period included Stephen Schaefer as Chairman (Riverstone alumni, GenOn Holdings, Golar LNG), Joseph Nigro (ex-Exelon CFO, ex-Constellation CEO, Blackstone Energy Transition Senior Advisor), Anthony Horton (ex-Energy Future Holdings CFO), Karen Hyde (retired Xcel Energy SVP/CCO; ex-DOD nuclear engineer), Gizman Abbas (ex-Apollo, ex-Goldman Sachs, NYISO board), and Christine Benson Schwartzstein (ex-Goldman Sachs 17 years, Apollo Infrastructure Company).

## The Amazon AWS PPA (March 2024 → June 2025 expansion)

In March 2024 Talen signed an initial power purchase agreement with Amazon Web Services to supply Susquehanna nuclear-generated electricity to Amazon's adjacent data center campus in Salem Township, Pennsylvania. The initial deal made Talen one of the first IPPs to monetise a behind-the-meter or near-meter nuclear-to-data-center electricity model.

In June 2025 the relationship was expanded materially: up to 1,920 MW of carbon-free nuclear power through 2042, with a ramp schedule potentially reaching the full volume by 2032, and delivery rights extending to other Amazon sites throughout Pennsylvania. The expansion included an option for Talen and Amazon to co-develop Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) within Talen's Pennsylvania footprint.

The Amazon expansion is among the largest single carbon-free data center PPAs in US history. The contract structurally repositions Talen as a long-term-contracted hyperscaler electricity supplier rather than a pure PJM merchant generator.

## The fleet acquisitions (2025)

Through 2025 Talen executed an accelerated M&A program adding gas-fired capacity:

- **Freedom and Guernsey** closed in FY2025 for approximately $3.8 billion — adding 2.8 GW of natural gas combined-cycle generation
- **Cornerstone** agreed at approximately $3.45 billion — adding 2.0 GW of combined-cycle plus 456 MW of peaking capacity

Total gas capacity additions: approximately 5.3 GW for ~$7.25 billion. The acquisition program is financed in part through new debt issuance: $1.4 billion of 6.250% senior unsecured notes due 2034 plus $1.3 billion of 6.500% senior unsecured notes due 2036. Management targets net leverage around 3.5x or lower over the cycle.

## RMR contracts and PJM capacity (2025)

In 2025 Talen secured reliability-must-run (RMR) contracts on the Brandon Shores ($145 million annually) and H.A. Wagner ($35 million annually) plants, with both starting June 2025 and running through May 2029. The RMR contracts add $180 million of fixed annual revenue through the contract terms.

In the 2027/2028 PJM capacity auction, Talen cleared 8,745 MW at $333.44/MW-day — implying approximately $1.06 billion in annual capacity payments at that price level on those cleared MWs.

## The December 2025 management realignment

In December 2025 Talen announced a strategic management realignment effective December 15, 2025:
- **Terry Nutt** elevated to President (from prior CFO role)
- **Cole Muller** named Chief Financial Officer (from prior EVP Strategic Ventures role)

The realignment formalised the operating-and-financial leadership split as Talen scaled through the AWS PPA delivery ramp plus the Cornerstone acquisition closing.

## FY2025 financial results

For the full fiscal year 2025 Talen reported:
- Adjusted EBITDA $1,035 million
- Adjusted FCF $524 million
- 13.1 GW total generation capacity (including Freedom + Guernsey close)
- Susquehanna generated ~17 TWh at ~$27/MWh

## Where the story sits at snapshot (May 23, 2026)

Talen Energy is two-and-a-half years into post-Chapter-11 public-company life and has executed a comprehensive operational and commercial transformation: long-term carbon-free contracted revenue with Amazon, accelerated gas-capacity M&A, RMR + capacity-market revenue layers, and a board + executive team built from the deepest EFH / GenOn / Constellation / Exelon / Apollo / Goldman pedigree set in the IPP universe. The investment proposition is the intersection of (a) AI data center electricity demand, (b) Susquehanna nuclear at ~$27/MWh LCOE monetising at hyperscaler-PPA contract prices, and (c) PJM capacity-market tightening driving meaningful sustained baseload returns.
