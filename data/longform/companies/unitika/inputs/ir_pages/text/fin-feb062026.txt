1. Overview of Business Performance, etc.
  (1) Overview of quarterly business performance
      During the first nine months of the consolidated fiscal year under review (April 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025),
      the domestic economy remained resilient, particularly in the manufacturing sector, driven by vigorous global
      AI-related demand, in addition to continued robust capital investment. Meanwhile, the outlook for the domestic
      economy remains unclear due to uncertainties in trade policy, exchange rate fluctuations, and geopolitical risks,
      including Japan-China relations.
      Under these circumstances, in accordance with its business restructuring plan, the UNITIKA Group steadily
      implemented structural reforms, such as withdrawal from unprofitable businesses, including business transfer.
      Furthermore, the Group continued to take the following measures to enhance profitability: promoting
      cost-reduction initiatives such as expense cuts, price revisions, and expanding sales of high-value-added
      functional products.
      As a result, in the first nine-month period of the consolidated fiscal year under review, net sales increased to
      95,637 million yen (up 2.2% year on year). Operating profit amounted to 9,030 million yen (up 110.3% year on
      year) due to the suspension of unprofitable sales and implementation of price revisions and cost reduction
      measures. In addition to increased operating profit, the progress of yen depreciation resulted in the posting of
      foreign exchange valuation gains of 940 million yen on foreign currency-denominated assets. Consequently,
      ordinary profit increased to 8,802 million yen (up 75.8% year on year). Additionally, in line with the withdrawal
      from unprofitable businesses, including business transfer, the Group recorded a gain on the sale of non-current
      assets of 23,661 million yen as extraordinary income. On the other hand, as a result of recording business
      restructuring expenses of 12,391 million yen as an extraordinary loss, profit attributable to owners of parent was
      10,649 million yen (versus loss attributable to owners of parent of 24,399 million yen in the same period of the
      previous year).
      Here is an overview of the business results by segment.

 [Polymers Segment]
     The Polymers Segment recorded generally steady sales throughout the period due to continued strong demand in
     the electronic materials field. In addition, profitability improved due to cost-reduction measures and product price
     revisions.
     In the Film business, sales of both nylon films and polyester films grew steadily in the packaging sector. In
     particular, sales of EMBLEM-HG, a high gas barrier nylon film, remained strong. In the industrial sector, sales
     remained solid due to continuous growth in electronic material-related demand. Meanwhile, in the overseas
     market, although sales were affected by competition from low-priced products, profitability improved
     significantly due to a shift in sales strategy as well as cost reduction measures. As a result, the Film business saw
     net sales decrease and operating profit increase.
     In the Plastic business, overall sales of engineering plastic materials increased, driven by steady growth in sales
     for electric and electronic component applications, despite weaker sales for automotive applications. Sales of
     specialty polymers also grew steadily for electric and electronic component applications. The Plastics business as
     a whole achieved growth in both net sales and operating profit supported by continued and focused efforts toward
     cost reduction and price revisions since the previous year.
     Consequently, the Polymers Segment achieved growth in both net sales and operating profit, with net sales
     reaching 42,476 million yen (up 0.7% year on year) and operating profit reaching 7,543 million yen (up 67.0%
     year on year).

 [Performance Materials Segment]
     The Performance Materials Segment saw a growth in sales across a wide range of applications, primarily in the
     electronic materials field. In addition, as a result of continuing the suspension of unprofitable sales and product
     price revisions, profitability improved.
     In the Activated Carbon Fibers business, the overseas sales market for VOC removal sheets for air purification
     applications was on a recovery trend, and sales for water purification applications, which are a mainstay of this
     business, remained strong.
     In the Glass Fibers business, sales in the industrial materials sector remained generally robust, chiefly driven by
     demand for building materials applications, including tents and sheets. In the electronic materials sector, driven by
     expanding semiconductor demand, sales grew significantly for ultra-thin Low CTE Glass Fabrics and ultra-thin E
     Glass Fabrics for mobile memory applications used for high-end mobile terminals.
     In the Glass Beads business, sales volume for road applications declined due to a continued decrease in the
     number of road construction projects and price competition from low-priced overseas products. In contrast, sales
     for industrial applications and reflective materials remained steady.
     In the Non-woven Fabrics business, spunbond non-woven fabrics sold well mainly as agricultural materials and
     for automotive applications. Overall sales of cotton spunlace non-woven fabrics remained steady, despite a decline
     in sales for consumer product applications, thanks to strong performance in industrial material applications.
     In the Industrial Fibers business, sales volume of polyester staple fibers decreased while sales volume of
     high-strength polyester yarns increased. Sales of monofilament remained steady.
     Consequently, the Performance Materials Segment achieved growth in both net sales and operating profit, with net
     sales reaching 29,144 million yen (up 5.8% year on year) and operating profit reaching 1,441 million yen (up

[Fibers and Textiles Segment]
    In the Garments, Lifestyle Materials and Bedding business, demand in the uniform sector, a mainstay of this
    business, remained steady, supported mainly by public-sector demand. On the other hand, sales volume decreased
    in the general clothing sector, including women’s clothing, and the bedding and sports clothing sectors due to
    sluggish demand. In the overseas business, sales of denim fabric remained solid. In the Industrial Materials
    business, sales for civil engineering applications and household products were strong, supported by the recovery
    in market conditions.
    Consequently, the Fibers & Textiles Segment achieved growth in both net sales and operating profit, with net
    sales reaching 23,944 million yen (up 0.8% year on year) and operating profit reaching 50 million yen (versus an
    operating loss of 560 million yen in the previous year).

[Other Segment]

   previous year) on net sales of 71 million yen (up 47.7% year on year).

2. Decrease of share capital and legal capital surplus
   Considering its current business structure and financial performance, the Company seeks to ensure financial
   soundness through the appropriate application of tax and institutional measures. In addition, it will conduct a
   reduction in share capital and legal capital surplus to prepare for future capital policies that support more agile
   and efficient operations.
   (1) Amount of share capital to be reduced
       The Company will reduce 10,000,838,268 yen from 10,100,838,268 yen, the amount of share capital after
       a capital increase through a third-party allotment (which consists of the total of share capital at the end of
       the previous consolidated fiscal year of 100,450,000 yen and the share capital increased through a
       third-party allotment of 10,000,388,268 yen), bringing the total share capital to 100,000,000 yen.
   (2) Amount of legal capital surplus to be reduced
       The Company will reduce 10,025,500,768 yen from 10,025,500,768 yen, the amount of legal capital
       surplus after a capital increase through a third-party allotment (which consists of the total of legal capital
       surplus at the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year of 25,112,500 yen and the legal capital surplus
       increased through a third-party allotment of 10,000,388,268 yen), bringing the total legal capital surplus to
       0 yen.
