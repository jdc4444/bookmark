# REVIC support decision for Unitika
Source: REVIC official page + Japanese news synthesis
Decision date: 2024-11-28
Investment decision date: 2025-02-13
Capital completion: 2025-04-30

## REVIC's commitment

- **Capital contribution**: ¥20B (¥200億, third-party allotment Class C preferred)
- **Financing line**: ¥15B (¥150億, loan separate from equity)
- **Total REVIC commitment**: ~¥35B
- **Bank debt forgiveness**: up to ¥43B (MUFG, Mizuho, Sumitomo Mitsui Trust)

## Scope of restructuring

Unitika exits:
- Apparel textiles
- Non-woven fabrics
- Industrial fiber businesses

Concentrates on:
- Polymer films (food packaging — EMBLEM HG retort barrier)
- Functional materials (semiconductor-grade glass cloth, activated carbon fibers)

## Business transfer destinations — five companies named

Within ~15 months of the Nov 2024 announcement, all major business transfer destinations have been determined:

1. **Seiren Co.** (3569.T) — Okazaki textile business (joint with Nippon Ester split)
2. **Shikibo Ltd.** (3109.T) — fiber-business portion
3. **Kawabo** — fiber-business portion (name spelling needs verification — could be Kawashima Selkon or Kawabo Co.)
4. **Zuiko Corporation** (6279.T) — spunlace cotton nonwovens (likely via COTEX subsidiary)
5. **Boken Quality Evaluation Organization** (ボーケン品質評価機構) — fiber-quality-evaluation related operations

**Not yet sold**: TUSCO (Unitika's Thailand polyester spunbond facility)

## Performance recovery

Per the same source narrative:
- FY3/26 OP guidance ¥9.5B = approximately **2.1x the prior year's ¥4.5B core**
- Core revenue now from polymer (food packaging nylon film) and functional materials (semiconductor-grade glass cloth)
