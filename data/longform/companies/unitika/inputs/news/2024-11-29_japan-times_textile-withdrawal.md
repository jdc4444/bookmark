# Unitika announces withdrawal from textiles
Source: Japan Times + Reuters via search
Date: 2024-11-29

## Headline event
Unitika announced exit from fiber and textile businesses, kicking off the formal restructuring under REVIC.

## Financial mechanics
- **Lenders waiving ¥30-40 billion**:
  - MUFG Bank
  - Mizuho Bank
  - Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Bank
- **REVIC acquires ~¥20 billion of preferred stock** with voting rights → REVIC becomes majority voter
- 2014 Thailand-production-shift restructuring deemed unsuccessful → drove new plan

## Strategic re-focus
- Exit fiber/textile (low-margin, weak yen exposure)
- Focus on food-packaging films and polymer products growing in SE Asia
- High-margin specialty: semiconductor, medical, environmental

## Follow-up: nonwovens sale (October 2025)
Source: Nonwovens Industry magazine (Oct 2025 issue)
- Unitika **found a buyer** for the nonwovens business
- Part of the larger fiber+textile exit
