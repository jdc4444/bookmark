# Unitika FY3/2025 Earnings Report Summary
Source: logmi Finance (https://finance.logmi.jp/articles/382024)
Event: 2025-05-27 earnings call
Published: 2025-06-03

## Headline numbers

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ¥126.4B | ¥118.3B | +6.8% |
| Operating Profit | ¥5.9B | ¥(2.5)B | +¥8.3B (flipped to profit) |
| Ordinary Profit | ¥4.7B | ¥(1.0)B | +¥5.7B |
| Net Loss (Parent) | ¥(24.3)B | ¥(5.4)B | larger loss |

Net loss driven by **¥37.9B impairment charge on fixed assets**.

## OP bridge: -¥2.5B → +¥5.9B (+¥8.3B)

- Pricing & product mix: +¥2.4B (discontinued unprofitable SE Asia sales; pricing reforms)
- Sales volume: +¥1.8B (domestic food packaging + materials recovery)
- Cost factors: +¥2.0B (depreciation decline post-impairment; cost-down initiatives)
- Raw materials: -¥0.6B (yen weakness offset commodity stability)

## Segments — three reportable segments

### High Polymer Business
- Revenue **¥55.4B** (+8.5%)
- OP **¥6.0B** (vs ¥0.6B PY)
- Film segment recovered on food packaging demand
- Engineering plastics weak in auto applications
- Functional resins strong growth

### Functional Materials Business
- Revenue **¥37.0B** (+8.3%)
- OP **¥0.3B** (vs ¥(2.5)B loss)
- Electronic materials recovered on **AI/data center semiconductor demand**
- Glass fiber + beads showed strength

### Fiber Business
- Revenue **¥33.9B** (+2.8%)
- OL **¥(0.357)B** (vs ¥(0.523)B prior — improving)
- Uniform demand solid, general apparel weak, global denim recovered

## REVIC restructuring (April 30, 2025)

- ¥20B third-party allotment to REVIC
- REVIC now holds **66.76% voting rights** via Class C preferred shares
- Board reconstituted: 7 outside directors + 1 outside auditor
- Maximum **¥43.0B debt forgiveness** approved by financial institutions

## Restructuring actions (target deadline Aug 2025)

- Divest apparel fiber, non-woven, and most industrial fiber operations
- Discontinue Emblem Asia line #4 (large nylon film) — completed, facility sale underway
- Shift polyester chip production to external sourcing (Okazaki plant closure planned)
- Optimize logistics routes and warehouse contracts

## Growth products

- **Emblem HG** (retort-compatible barrier film): double-digit % growth trajectory
- **UniPeel** (silicone-free release film): semiconductor manufacturing adoption expanding
- Glass fiber ultra-thin and thermally-stable grades — targeting chip substrates
- Transparent flame-retardant sheet — **>50% domestic market share** in smoke curtains

## Pipeline development

- Semiconductor packaging
- Medical devices
- Environmental applications

## Management commentary (CEO Fujii Minoru)

- "Price revisions and cost-reduction measures"
- "Margin recovery in food packaging drove polymer segment growth"
- Acknowledged "persistent price competition in SE Asia"

## Forward guidance

- **FY3/26 (current FY)**: outlook withheld — "reasonable forecast calculation currently difficult due to business divestiture execution uncertainty"

- **FY3/29 5-year targets**:
  - Revenue **¥70B** (down from current via divestitures — "shrink to grow")
  - OP **¥6.5B**
  - Strategic focus: high-margin polymer + inorganic materials (fibers, ceramics)
