# DIC × Unitika joint PPS film for mmWave applications
Source: DIC press release via BusinessWire (CES 2026 announcement, Dec 2025)

## Product
DIC Corporation developed a **new specialty polyphenylene sulfide (PPS) film in collaboration with Unitika Ltd.** that suppresses transmission loss at high frequencies.

## Application
- Low dielectric properties
- Target: **millimeter-wave printed circuit boards** compatible with next-generation communications devices
- Also targets **millimeter-wave radar**

## Why this matters for the dossier
- Confirms Unitika's specialty-materials thesis is materializing in real partnerships
- 5G/6G millimeter-wave + automotive radar are growing TAMs
- Validates the "polymer → semiconductor & advanced electronics" pivot
- Joint venture / co-development with DIC (a much larger Japanese specialty chemicals firm) is itself a credibility marker
