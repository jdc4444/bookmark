### $BOXX — Alpha Architect 1-3 Month Box ETF  *(NYSE Arca)*

Alpha Architect 1-3 Month Box ETF seeks to replicate T-bill equivalent returns through box spreads — a four-legged options strategy (long call, short put at one strike; long put, short call at a higher strike) that eliminates market-directional risk and produces a synthetic risk-free rate. The fund does not hold actual Treasury bills. Under IRC Section 1256, gains from the strategy receive 60/40 treatment (60% long-term, 40% short-term capital gains) rather than being taxed as ordinary income, as T-bill interest would be. For investors in top federal brackets, the tax advantage can be substantial.

**Recent.** The strategy's tax treatment is contested: IRC Section 1258 (the anti-conversion statute) may allow the IRS to recharacterize gains as ordinary income. As of mid-2026, the IRS has not acted against the fund, which has approximately $11 billion in assets at a 0.19% expense ratio. BOXX is state-tax-disadvantaged relative to T-bill ETFs, whose interest is state-exempt; investors in high-tax states should weigh this carefully.
